🗑️ Account Deletion Policy

Find Local - Find Local Services

📋How to Delete Your Account

Follow these simple steps to permanently delete your account:

  1. Open the Find Local app on your device
  2. Navigate to the Home Page
  3. Add at least one domain detail to your profile
  4. Once added, the "Deregister Completely" button will appear on the Home Page
  5. Click on the "Deregister Completely" button
  6. Confirm your decision to proceed with account deletion

✅ Your account will be deregistered from all active domains and all associated data will be permanently deleted from our servers.

ℹ️ About Domain Registration

What are domains? Domains represent different service categories or areas where you can register your services (such as plumbing, electrical work, carpentry, etc.).

Complete Deregistration: The "Deregister Completely" button removes your account from all domains you've registered in, ensuring complete account deletion.

⚠️ Important Notice

This action is irreversible.

Once you click "Deregister Completely":

  • You will be deregistered from all active domains simultaneously
  • All your profile information across all domains will be permanently removed
  • Your service listings will be immediately removed from all categories
  • You will need to register again to use the app
  • Your phone number will be unlinked from our system
  • Your location data will be completely erased

🗄️Data We Store

ℹ️ Information Collected

Domain Details: Service categories and areas where you've registered to offer your services

Service Information: Skills and services you provide across different domains (if registered as service provider)

All data is used solely for app functionality and is never shared with third parties. When you deregister completely, all this information is permanently deleted from every domain

Service Information: Skills and services you provide (if registered as service provider)

All data is used solely for app functionality and is never shared with third parties.

🛡️Your Privacy Matters

By deleting your account, you acknowledge and agree to the permanent removal of your data as described above.

We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. All data deletion is processed immediately and cannot be recovered.
